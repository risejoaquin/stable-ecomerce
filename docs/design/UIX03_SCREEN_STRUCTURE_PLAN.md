# UIX03 — Screen Structure Plan

## Home
- Full-width editorial hero.
- Navigation compacta superior.
- Product grid con 4–8 destacados.
- Campaign/Lookbook block.
- Skincare education strip.
- Trust + shipping + payment clarity.

## Catalog
- Heading editorial.
- Category tabs.
- Product grid 2 columns mobile / 4 columns desktop.
- Minimal filters.
- No visual clutter.

## Product Detail
- Large image gallery.
- Sticky purchase block on desktop or sticky CTA on mobile.
- Benefits in short bullets.
- Accordions for ingredients, usage, shipping, returns.
- Related products.

## Landing Pages
- Campaign hero.
- Editorial copy.
- Featured products.
- Routine bundle section.
- CTA sections.

## Cart
- Simple drawer/page.
- Product line clarity.
- Promo/coupon if applicable.
- Trust microcopy.

## Checkout
- One clear path.
- Fewer distractions.
- Stripe/payment trust.
- Summary visible.

## Mobile
- Bottom navigation or compact menu.
- Big tap targets.
- Product image-first.
- Sticky Add to Cart.
